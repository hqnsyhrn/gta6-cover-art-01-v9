Velvet Coast — a GTA-style cover art studio that turns your own photos into a six-panel "VELVET COAST" poster: a giant chrome title over the photos, a pink neon script, a violet sunset grade, and a full cast with role labels. Add photos from your machine (button or drag-and-drop onto any panel), crop and reposition each one with sliders, edit the title, script, captions and grade, then export the poster as a PNG in 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080. Everything runs offline in the browser; photos never leave the machine. All people on the cover are original characters; no Rockstar Games logo, artwork or font is used.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
